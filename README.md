📊 Just wrapped up a Sales and Refund Dashboard in Power BI, and the numbers tell a story.

I analysed a full year of orders (4,286) to understand where money was walking out the door through returns.

What I found:
🔴 18.48% return rate, about 3.82M in refunds
📈 Net revenue grew from 1.34M to 2.54M, but returns spiked just as fast in Q4 (137 return orders in December vs ~50 earlier in the year)
👕 Fashion and Electronics account for about 60% of all returns
📏 "Wrong size" dominates apparel and footwear, while damaged and defective items hit kitchen and electronics
👖 One product had a 35% return rate, nearly double the average

What I'd recommend: better size guidance, tighter packaging and supplier QC, and a Q4 returns plan.

The dashboard has two pages: an executive overview (KPIs, revenue trend, channel mix) and a deep dive (refund trends, category breakdown, return reason heatmap by subcategory).

Tools: Power BI, DAX, data modelling.
